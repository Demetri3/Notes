# 📑 Notes: Capture Life in Color
Notes is a minimalist application for those who want true simplicity. Built with the latest Android technologies, it combines a sleek, grid-based design with an intuitive user experience that adapts perfectly to any device.

Features:

## 🌈 Vibrant Staggered Grid
Break away from boring lists. Notes displays your thoughts in a dynamic, colorful staggered grid that gives you a visual overview of your workspace at a glance.


## 📅 Smart Organization
Never lose track of when an idea struck. The app automatically categorizes your notes into unobtrusive time-based fields:
• This Week: Focus on your immediate tasks and recent thoughts.
• Last Month: Revisit your recent history.
• Older: Dive into your archived archive of brilliance.


## 🔍 Reactive Real-Time Search
Finding your notes is instantaneous. As you type in the animated search bar, the app filters through titles and content in real-time, highlighting exactly what you need.


## 🌓 Theme Your Way
Whether you're a night owl or a morning person, we've got you covered. Access the Settings Drawer to quickly toggle between:
• Light Mode: Crisp, clean, and professional.
• Dark Mode: Deep, immersive, and easy on the eyes.
• System Default: Let your device decide for you.


## 📱 The Adaptive Experience
Notes is built with an Adaptive-First philosophy. Using modern "List-Detail" scaffolding:
• On Phones: Enjoy a focused, single-pane experience that transitions smoothly between your list and your editor.
• On Tablets & Foldables: The app automatically expands into a dual-pane layout, allowing you to browse your collection on the left while editing on the right.


## 🎨 Design Excellence
• Fluid Motion: Every transition is powered by custom fade-and-scale animations, making the app feel alive and responsive.
• Edge-to-Edge Experience: The UI flows beautifully behind the status and navigation bars, maximizing your screen real estate without overlapping system indicators.
• Modern Branding: Featuring a minimalist "Folded Pages" icon that symbolizes the layered depth of your thoughts.


## 🛠️ Built With (The Tech Stack)
For the developers and enthusiasts, Notes is powered by the cutting edge of the Android ecosystem:
• Jetpack Compose: For a fully declarative, modern UI.
• Navigation 3: Utilizing state-driven navigation for seamless screen transitions.
• Room Persistence: High-performance local storage ensuring your notes are always available offline.
• Material 3 Adaptive: To ensure the layout scales perfectly across all form factors.
• Kotlin Coroutines: For fast, non-blocking database and UI operations.


## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Download: Grab the latest APK from the Releases section.
2. Install: Open the APK on your Android device (ensure "Install from Unknown Sources" is enabled).
3. Create: Tap the + button and start taking notes!
