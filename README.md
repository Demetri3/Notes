# Notes
This is my first android application

Notes is a simple application built with the latest Android technologies using kotlin, design is based off samsungs OneUI theming, grid-based design that should to any device.


## Grid
Break away from boring lists. Notes displays your thoughts in a dynamic, colorful staggered grid that gives you a visual overview of your workspace at a glance.


## Time-Based Organization
Never lose track of when an idea struck. The app automatically categorizes your notes into unobtrusive time-based fields:

• This Week: Focus on your immediate tasks and recent thoughts.

• Last Month: Revisit your recent history.

• Older: Dive into your archived archive of brilliance.


## Search
Finding your notes is instantaneous. As you type in the animated search bar, the app filters through titles and content in real-time, highlighting exactly what you need.


## Themes
• Light Mode: Crisp, clean, and professional.

• Dark Mode: Deep, immersive, and easy on the eyes.

• System Default: Let your device decide for you.



## How to Use
1. Download: Grab the latest APK from the Releases section.
   
2. Install: Open the APK on your Android device (ensure "Install from Unknown Sources" is enabled).
   
3. Create: Tap the + button and start taking notes
